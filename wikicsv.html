What the parser understands

Full wikitext source is fine — everything outside a table is simply ignored, and multiple tables get stacked into one CSV.

SyntaxHow it's handled
{| … |}Table boundaries; text outside is skipped
|-Starts a new row (row attributes ignored)
! a !! bHeader cells — become the first CSV row(s)
| a || bData cells; several per line are supported
| colspan="2" | xValue kept once; spanned columns emitted as empty fields
| class="…" | xCell attributes stripped, content kept
|+ captionSkipped
Multi-line cellsFlattened onto one line
Nested {| … |}Flattened into the parent cell (you'll see a warning)
[[Page|label]], {{convert|3690|km}}Reduced to plain text when Clean markup is on
<ref>, HTML tags, commentsRemoved

CSV rules applied

  • Smart quoting: fields containing the delimiter, " or a line break are wrapped in quotes; inner quotes are doubled.
  • Quote all fields forces quotes around everything.
  • Pick comma, semicolon or tab (TSV) output.
  • Optional UTF-8 BOM so Excel detects accents correctly.
  • rowspan can't exist in CSV — the value stays in its first row and you get a heads-up.
